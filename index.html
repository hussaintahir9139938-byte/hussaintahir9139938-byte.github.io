Legal Tech · RAG

Stephen's Law LLM

Secure RAG knowledge platform for a law firm, designed around confidential documents and retrieval-based LLM answers.
Goal: let legal professionals ask questions in plain language and get accurate answers drawn from thousands of confidential case files.
Ingestion: an automated pipeline reads documents from the firm's NAS storage, applies OCR where needed, splits text into meaningful chunks, creates vector embeddings and stores them in Qdrant.
Answering: for each question it retrieves the most relevant context and uses an LLM to write a clear, human-readable response.

ArchitectureOCR → embeddings → Qdrant → retrieval → LLM
FocusDocument ingestion & secure knowledge retrieval
